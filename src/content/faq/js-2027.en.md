---
title: Will eCamp v3 be adapted to the new Y+S education system 2027?
---

Yes. We are currently working out a concept and discussing with the youth organizations how to best implement this. Among other things, we plan to provide camp templates, input fields and content blocks for the new Y+S rules. We collect wishes and decisions in [this GitHub issue](https://github.com/ecamp/ecamp3/issues/10734).<br/>
Since the new rules have not been finalized in every detail yet, we cannot guarantee what will be adapted by when. It will however likely take a few more months.<br/>
If you need to start planning your camp before 2027, you can set up a workaround before planning any activities: Create your camp using the Y+S camp template. Under Admin → Checklists, create an empty checklist "Erscheinungsformen" with one item per Erscheinungsform. Under Admin → Activities, delete "LS: Lagersport", rename "LA: Lageraktivität" to "J+S-Aktivität" and in its "Template for new activities", replace the LA thematic area with a "Checklist" block. You can enter the movement time into the "Location" field, e.g. "BZ 60min".
