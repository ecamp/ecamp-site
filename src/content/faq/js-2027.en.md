---
title: Does eCamp v3 support the new Y+S education system 2027?
---

Yes. There is a dedicated camp template for Y+S camps from 2027 onwards. With the "Y+S activity characteristics" content block, you can select the characteristics covered in each activity, and with the "Y+S physical activity time" content block, you can record the physical activity time for each schedule entry of an activity. The physical activity time can also be shown in the picasso.
