---
title: What will happen to eCamp v2?
---

The original eCamp version 2 can still be used read-only until spring 2027.
However, the code is difficult to maintain and thus we don't update it any longer.<br/>
No data will be migrated from v2 to v3.
We recommend that you download old camps that you want to keep as PDF files and archive them yourself, and switch to eCamp v3 as soon as possible.
